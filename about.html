About Us

Engineers Who Think in Copper & Code

CopperTrace Labs is an electronics design house focused on PCB design, embedded systems and IoT. We help startups, OEMs and researchers turn ideas into reliable, manufacturable hardware.

Our Story

Started on a Workbench

We began as a small group of electronics engineers who loved solving tricky hardware problems — noisy power rails, RF that wouldn't connect, batteries that died too soon.

Today we run a full design lab with schematic and layout, firmware development, SMT prototyping and test benches under one roof. We've delivered boards for agriculture, energy, industrial automation, healthcare and consumer products.

What hasn't changed: we care about every trace, every via, and every line of firmware that goes into your product.
