What We Do

Electronics Engineering, End to End

One team for hardware, firmware and connectivity — so your product works as a whole, not as a pile of parts.

PCB Design & Layout

Schematic capture, 2–8 layer layout, high-speed & RF routing, DFM/DFT checks and Gerber-ready output.

Embedded Firmware

Bare-metal & RTOS firmware for STM32, ESP32, nRF and AVR — drivers, bootloaders, OTA and low-power tuning.

IoT Solutions

Wi-Fi, BLE, LoRa & GSM connected devices with MQTT cloud dashboards and mobile app integration.

Prototyping & Testing

Rapid prototypes, SMT assembly, bring-up, functional testing and pre-compliance checks before production.
