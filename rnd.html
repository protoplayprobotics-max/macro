Research & Development

Where Ideas Get Wired Up

Our R&D lab explores new sensing, connectivity and power technologies — and turns them into proven building blocks for client projects and our own products.

Capabilities

Hardware Design Capabilities

Multi-layer PCB

2 to 8 layer stack-ups with controlled impedance and careful plane design.

  • Impedance-controlled routing
  • Blind / buried vias
  • Flex & rigid-flex

High-Speed & RF

USB, Ethernet, DDR and 2.4 GHz / Sub-GHz RF layouts done right.

  • Differential pair tuning
  • Antenna matching networks
  • EMI/EMC-aware layout

Power Electronics

Efficient, safe power stages for battery, solar and mains-powered devices.

  • SMPS & LDO design
  • Li-ion charging & BMS
  • Isolated AC-DC

Embedded Systems

Firmware for 8-bit to 32-bit MCUs with clean, maintainable architecture.

  • FreeRTOS / Zephyr
  • Secure bootloader & OTA
  • Ultra-low-power modes

IoT & Cloud

End-to-end connected systems from sensor node to dashboard.

  • MQTT / HTTP / CoAP
  • AWS IoT, ThingsBoard
  • Mobile app integration

Sensors & Analog

Precision signal chains for real-world measurements.

  • Low-noise amplifiers & ADCs
  • Ultrasonic, current, gas sensing
  • Calibration routines
